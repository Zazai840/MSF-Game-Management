Race time entry: five variants

Type 12.34 s and 1:32.40 in each. Then try: backspace over a separator, tap into the middle and change a digit, paste "1:32.40". The log shows what the browser sent.

A. Today: digits only, number pad, readout below

inputmode="numeric" pattern="[0-9]*". Last two digits are hundredths. The box shows exactly what was typed.







  
B. Left-to-right mask __:__.__ on the number pad

  
inputmode="numeric". Separators are inserted as you type, filling from the left (a typical input-mask library).

  
  






  
C. Decimal keypad, free text, strict check

  
inputmode="decimal". Accepts 12.34 or 1:32.40 (or 1.32.40 where no colon key exists); anything else is refused on Next.

  
  






  
D. Full keyboard, strict check

  
No inputmode. Accepts 12.34 or 1:32.40 only.

  
  






  
E. Right-to-left (calculator) fill on the number pad

  
inputmode="numeric". Digits shift in from the right; the box itself shows 0.01, 0.12, 1.23, 12.34. Backspace always removes the last digit.
